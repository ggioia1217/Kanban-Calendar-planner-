What's the same in every style

Layout, the visual clock, colors that mean something (amber due, red late or roadblock, green done or break) and the word styling.

What changes

Background, card shape and edges, font, how much each goal color tints its items, and whether bars are frosted.

Accessibility

Glass keeps blur on bars and cards only, never behind small text. Every style keeps text contrast at 4.5:1 or better.
