A · Soft timeline

Like Tiimo and Structured: each item is a colored bubble with an icon on a dotted time rail.

  • Time is something you can see. Gaps and breaks stand out
  • Calm, friendly colors suit anxious mornings
  • Icons let you scan without reading
  • Lots of color fights the urgency glow and status colors
  • Takes more space per task, so the week view gets long

B · Crisp paper

Like Things 3 (Apple Design Award) and Todoist: white, round checkboxes, color only where it means something.

  • Quietest screen, so the grammar styling reads best here
  • Fits the most tasks on one screen
  • Color stays meaningful: amber means due, red means late
  • Can feel plain, and may give less of a dopamine pull
  • Time is easy to forget without a visible timeline

C · Liquid glass

Apple's iOS 26 look: frosted, see-through layers with a floating pill tab bar and big rounded corners.

  • Feels native and current on iPhone and iPad
  • The floating bar and + button stay in reach without taking space
  • Text over blurry color is harder to read, a real risk for this audience
  • Heavy blur drains battery on fridge displays and older Android phones

D · Playful reward

Like Finch and Duolingo: chunky buttons that press down, a progress ring, streaks and stickers.

  • Every tap feels like a win, the strongest dopamine pull
  • Buttons that physically press in make "done" feel real
  • Streaks can shame people on bad days, which clashes with light days
  • Can feel childish at work and in front of a manager

My pick: crisp paper as the base, borrowing one thing from each

A quiet base keeps the grammar styling, urgency glow and status colors readable. The other styles each add one thing that works for ADHD.

Calm white base

Plain white surfaces and thin lines. Color only means something: due, late, break, now. More tasks fit on screen.

From B: Things 3
Hanging times and icon tiles

Upcoming items hang off a time column, each with a small colored icon tile for its goal, so you can scan without reading.

From A: Tiimo, Structured
Goal colors

Each goal picks a color and icon once. Its tasks carry that tile everywhere: board, flows, notifications.

From A
Floating tab bar and + button

A frosted pill bar that floats above content and shrinks while you scroll. Glass only on bars, never behind text.

From C: iOS 26
Pressable reward buttons

Complete and other win moments get a chunky button that presses in, plus the celebration. Everything else stays flat.

From D: Duolingo, Finch
Round checkboxes, bigger radius

Round checks for steps, 20px corners on the Now card, and a softer, larger Now card that reads as "the one thing."

From B and C
No streaks that break

Keep progress rings and lifetime counts. Skip streaks that reset to zero; they punish bad days.

Lesson from D
Same idea at night

Dark mode uses deep blue-grey, not black, with the same goal colors made a little brighter.
